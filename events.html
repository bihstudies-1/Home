Angkor Wat

Dr. Vasudha Narayanan

Distinguished Professor, University of Florida

A remarkable evening with Dr. Vasudha Narayanan, whose presentation brought the rich history and symbolism of Angkor Wat to life through art, storytelling, and accessible scholarship.

The event brought neighbors, scholars, and guests together in an atmosphere of warmth and curiosity.

Recent Event

The Remarkable Life, Work, and Influence of Ramanujan

Prof. Krishnaswami Alladi

Department of Mathematics, University of Florida

BIHS welcomed Prof. Krishnaswami Alladi for a special presentation on the life and legacy of Srinivasa Ramanujan, one of history's most extraordinary mathematical minds.

Prof. Alladi shared insights into Ramanujan's remarkable mathematical contributions and his continuing influence on modern mathematics.

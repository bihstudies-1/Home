Faculty & Curriculum

Six Units · Five Scholars · One Integrated Program

Each unit approaches questions of consciousness and reality from a different philosophical or scientific perspective.

Unit 1

Vedic Cosmology

Dr. Tiziano Valentinuzzi

  • Crisis of meaning: physicalism and questions of human purpose
  • Consciousness-based universe: ātmā-centered worldview, neuroscience, and panpsychism
  • Simulation hypothesis: Vedic metaphysics, quantum physics, and information theory
  • Bhakti and reason: examining divine love as a rational principle
Unit 2

Philosophy Foundations

Dr. Shivanand Sharma

  • Argumentation: logical reasoning and debate methodology
  • Philosophy of Mind: consciousness and the architecture of thought
  • Philosophy of Science: scientific methodology and its limitations
  • Philosophy of Religion: theological and spiritual epistemology
Unit 3

Foundations of Metaphysics

Dr. Howard Resnick

  • Central inquiry: Can there be a true spiritual science?
  • Examining metaphysical questions at the intersection of philosophy, science, and spiritual knowledge
Unit 4

Physics and Cosmology

Dr. Doug Watson

  • Crisis in Physics: conceptual limitations in modern physics
  • Space, Time, and Matter: quantum mechanics and foundational assumptions
  • Quantum Gravity: emerging relational approaches to reality
  • Science and Philosophy: relational cosmology and consciousness
Unit 5

Consciousness & Matter

Akhandadhi Das

  • Consciousness: the “hard problem of consciousness”
  • Reality: metaphysical foundations and consciousness–world interaction
Unit 6

Life, Cosmos & Beyond

Akhandadhi Das

  • Life: biological mysteries and scientific perspectives related to Purāṇic accounts
  • Cosmos: consciousness-based universal structure and realms of free consciousness

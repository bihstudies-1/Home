Beginning Fall Quarter 2026, BIHS offers three structured academic courses designed to university standards. These courses are part of BIHS's ongoing effort to build a rigorous academic program in preparation for formal accreditation. Each course runs 10 weeks, meets three hours per week, and is taught by doctoral faculty. Students who complete course requirements receive a BIHS Certificate of Completion.

Fall Quarter 2026 — Course Offerings

CONS 101 — Biology and Consciousness

An exploration of the relationship between biological science and consciousness, examining materialist and non-materialist frameworks through neuroscience, philosophy of biology, and Vedic perspectives on life and awareness.

HIST 101 — History of the Vaishnava Tradition in the West

A historical survey of the transmission and development of Vaishnava philosophy from its South Asian origins to its establishment as a global intellectual movement.

PHIL 101 — Introduction to Philosophy

A systematic introduction to epistemology, metaphysics, ethics, and philosophy of mind drawing on both Western and Indian philosophical traditions.

Public Lecture Series

In Summer 2025, BIHS hosted the Summer Discovery Series — a free three-weekend online symposium featuring 19 distinguished scholars from around the world exploring consciousness, ethics, cosmology, and philosophy of science. The series attracted over 300 participants internationally and demonstrated BIHS's capacity to convene rigorous academic dialogue across disciplines.

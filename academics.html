Academic Catalog

This document represents BIHS's academic planning framework in preparation for our Florida State License application. It includes upcoming course descriptions, learning outcomes, faculty qualifications, participation guidelines, and program policies for Fall Quarter 2026.

Download Academic Catalog (PDF)

Beginning Fall Quarter 2026, BIHS offers three structured educational programs designed to university standards, each running 10 weeks and taught by doctoral faculty. Each program meets three hours per week and follows a rigorous curriculum with assignments and a final assessment. Participants who complete all requirements receive a BIHS Certificate of Completion in recognition of participation — not an academic credential or postsecondary certificate. These programs are being developed with accreditation in mind as part of BIHS's journey toward becoming a fully licensed institution. Be part of BIHS from the beginning.

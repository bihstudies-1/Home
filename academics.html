Academics

The Bhaktivedanta Institute for Higher Studies offers university-style courses at the intersection of science, philosophy, and the study of consciousness. Beginning Fall Quarter 2026, BIHS launches its first formal academic term with three credit-bearing courses delivered online via live Zoom and Canvas.

Fall Quarter 2026 — Course Offerings

CONS 101 — Biology and Consciousness

An exploration of the relationship between biological science and consciousness, examining materialist and non-materialist frameworks through neuroscience, philosophy of biology, and Vedic perspectives.

HIST 101 — History of the Vaishnava Tradition in the West

A historical survey of the transmission and development of Vaishnava philosophy and devotional tradition from its South Asian origins to its establishment as a global intellectual movement.

PHIL 101 — Introduction to Philosophy

A systematic introduction to epistemology, metaphysics, ethics, and philosophy of mind drawing on both Western and Indian philosophical traditions.

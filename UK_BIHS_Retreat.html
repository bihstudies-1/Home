Executive Overview

The Bhaktivedanta Institute for Higher Studies (BIHS) proposes to host its first major conference retreat in Europe at the Centro Studi Bhaktivedanta institute in Italy. This strategic event will bring together devotee scholars, researchers, and leaders to engage with the important intersection of Krishna consciousness and modern science.

Strategic Location

Centro Studi Bhaktivedanta in Italy provides an ideal venue with excellent accommodation facilities and a contemplative atmosphere conducive to scholarly exchange.

Scalable Format

The retreat is designed to accommodate 50 participants, with flexibility to increase.

Mission Alignment

This initiative directly supports BIHS's core mission of advancing scholarly understanding of Krishna consciousness in dialogue with contemporary science.

Value Proposition

Proposed venue delivers exceptional value for the retreat's strategic importance in comparison to main alternatives.

Theme & Strategic Goals

Conference Theme:

Krishna Consciousness in the Age of Science: Promoting Informed Engagement with Modern Science for the Future of Śrīla Prabhupāda's Movement

Five Strategic Goals

1. Awareness, Identity, and Reputation

To raise awareness of the Bhaktivedanta Institute for Higher Studies (BIHS) among devotees and strengthen understanding of its strategies for enhancing ISKCON's reputation and influence in wider society. This goal establishes BIHS as a thought leader within the movement and demonstrates its commitment to advancing Krishna consciousness through rigorous scholarship.

2. Cultural Positioning on Science

To promote a balanced and constructive response to science within ISKCON, replacing fear and resistance with informed engagement. By fostering dialogue between traditional spiritual wisdom and contemporary scientific inquiry, this goal helps devotees develop a sophisticated understanding that honors both dimensions of knowledge.

3. Theological and Scientific Integration

To clarify the Bhagavatam's vision in relation to modern science and develop a rigorous, thoughtful interface between the two. This involves detailed scholarly exploration of how Vedic philosophy addresses contemporary questions in cosmology, biology, consciousness studies, and other scientific domains.

4. Research Community and Scholarly Development

To build a sustainable network of devotee researchers and create opportunities for participation and collaboration on research projects grounded in the Bhagavatam. This fosters intellectual community, enables knowledge sharing, and establishes pathways for ongoing scholarly collaboration beyond the retreat.

5. Education and Outreach Impact

To equip learners, teachers and institutions to engage confidently and competently with modern science in relation to Bhāgavata theology, and to translate research and scholarship into tangible outputs that support saṅkīrtana outreach and the wider mission.

About the Venue

Centro Studi Bhaktivedanta, Ponsacco (Pisa), Italy

The Centro Studi Bhaktivedanta (CSB) is a dedicated center for philosophical study and spiritual practice located in Ponsacco, Pisa. This world-class retreat facility provides an ideal environment for the BIHS Europe Retreat 2027.

Suitability

Mission Alignment
  • Accredited training institution under Italian Ministry of Education and Merit (MIM) Directive 170/2016
  • Purpose-built facility dedicated to philosophical study and spiritual practice aligned with BIHS mission
  • Proven track record hosting international scholarly and spiritual gatherings
Facilities and Environment
  • Academic Spaces: Appropriate meeting and seminar spaces for scholarly presentations and group discussions. Main Lecture Hall (130 m², 80 seats), three classrooms (130 m², 60 m², 40 m²), all equipped with air conditioning, screens, projectors, microphones, and sound systems.
  • Contemplative Atmosphere: Serene environment conducive to deep intellectual and spiritual engagement, supporting both scholarly discourse and reflective practice
  • Dining: Prasadam provided on a full-board basis; plant-based and natural.
  • Wellness Facilities: Two fully equipped Ayurvedic treatment rooms; men's and women's changing rooms with toilets, showers, and hairdryers

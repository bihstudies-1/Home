Publishing the Conversation

Courses, lectures, research, and conferences can spark important ideas. Publishing makes those ideas durable—available to readers long after a class or event has ended.

BIHS is developing a publication program that ranges from accessible books for general readers to more specialized scholarly work, while also preparing new titles directly from current teaching and research.

In Memoriam · October 2026

Remembering Michael A. Cremo (Drutakarma Dasa)

The Bhaktivedanta Institute for Higher Studies remembers with deep appreciation Michael A. Cremo (Drutakarma Dasa), whose lifelong dedication to exploring human origins, consciousness, and the relationship between science and spiritual knowledge has left an enduring legacy.

“Some people spend their lives asking questions others would rather leave alone.”

In a personal tribute, BIHS Executive Director Bob Cohen reflects on Cremo’s intellectual contributions, his decades of research, and the friendship and mentorship they shared.

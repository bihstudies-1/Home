For Personal Enrichment

BIHS educational programs are avocational and non-credit. They are offered for personal enrichment and do not confer college credit, academic degrees, or postsecondary credentials. A BIHS Certificate of Completion recognizes successful completion of stated course requirements and is not an academic credential.

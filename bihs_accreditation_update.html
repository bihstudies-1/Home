Institutional Update

Becoming a Licensed Institution — Here is What That Means for You

Our Six-Year Journey Toward Full Academic Accreditation

Something significant is happening at BIHS — and we want our community to be the first to know.

The Bhaktivedanta Institute for Higher Studies is preparing to apply for a Florida State License through the Commission for Independent Education — the first formal step toward becoming a fully accredited degree-granting institution. This is not a distant dream. This is an active plan with a clear timeline, and it is already underway.

Why This Matters

For years BIHS has offered rigorous scholarly programming — conferences, seminars, courses, and symposia — to a growing global community. What we are building now is something more permanent. A licensed, accredited institution where the intersection of science, philosophy, and Vedic wisdom is not just a conversation — it is a curriculum. A degree. A credential recognized by the academic world.

This means that one day — sooner than you might think — a student will be able to earn a Bachelor of Arts, a Master of Arts, or even a PhD in Consciousness Studies from the Bhaktivedanta Institute for Higher Studies. That student could be you. That student could be your child.

The Roadmap

Our path to full accreditation follows a clear and proven sequence:

Step 12026

Florida State License

We are preparing to apply this year to the Florida Commission for Independent Education. This license gives BIHS legal authority to operate as an official educational institution and issue certificates. We expect to have this in place by the end of 2026.

Step 22027–2028

DEAC Accreditation

The Distance Education Accrediting Commission is a US Department of Education recognized accreditor specializing in online institutions. DEAC accreditation gives BIHS national recognition and the authority to offer accredited online degrees.

Step 32029–2032

SACSCOC Regional Accreditation

The Southern Association of Colleges and Schools Commission on Colleges is the gold standard of academic accreditation in the American Southeast. SACSCOC accreditation opens the door to Bachelor's, Master's, and PhD programs fully recognized across the United States and internationally.

What We Have Already Built

We are not starting from scratch. BIHS already has:

  • Seven years of academic programming since 2019
  • 181 active participants in our current pilot program
  • 90% doctoral faculty — internationally recognized scholars
  • A full Canvas learning management system
  • A formal Academic Catalog designed to accreditation standards
  • A historic headquarters in Gainesville, Florida with a 6,000-volume research library
  • A clear institutional mission and governance structure

The foundation is already here. What we are doing now is formalizing it.

How You Can Help

This journey requires resources — faculty, technology, administration, and legal counsel. Every donation to BIHS is an investment in something that will outlast all of us — a permanent academic home for the study of consciousness, philosophy, and the Vedic tradition in the Western world.

"We are very close to applying for our state license. We keep working toward it every day. What we are building together will serve this community and the world for generations."

— Monica Dabos, PhD, Associate Director, BIHS

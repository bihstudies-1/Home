A Year in Motion

Books, gatherings, visiting scholars, training programs, and creative work: a few more moments from a remarkably active year at BIHS.

OPTIONAL HERO COLLAGE — Ratha Yatra · Sadhu Sanga · BIHS Gainesville · Art

Not every meaningful moment needs a full newsletter story. This page gathers a few glimpses of the programs, gatherings, visitors, and community connections that have also filled the year.

Los Angeles Ratha Yatra

BIHS joined the Los Angeles Ratha Yatra with a book table and a selection of publications, giving festival visitors an opportunity to discover work in science, philosophy, consciousness, cosmology, and Bhāgavata thought.

The event was also a reminder that publishing becomes meaningful when books leave the shelf and meet actual readers.

Sadhu Sanga

BIHS was also present at Sadhu Sanga, creating another opportunity to meet readers, reconnect with friends and supporters, and share the Institute’s educational and publication work in person.

BIHS also opened its Gainesville facility to host a guru training program—one example of the Institute serving not only through its own courses and research, but also as a place where serious educational and community programs can gather.

The Remarkable Life, Work, and Influence of Ramanujan

On April 17, BIHS welcomed Prof. Krishnaswami Alladi of the University of Florida for a special presentation on Srinivasa Ramanujan, one of the most extraordinary mathematical minds in modern history.

The evening brought the life of a great mathematician into the wider BIHS conversation about knowledge, creativity, intuition, and intellectual history.

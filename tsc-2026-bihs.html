One Example: The “Harder Problem”

Among the confirmed presentations, Michael Cremo addresses what he calls the boundary problem in consciousness studies: if consciousness is not adequately explained as a simple product of neural activity, why does an individual field of experience seem bounded at the scale of a particular living body?

His presentation places this contemporary problem in dialogue with Indian knowledge systems and Vedānta, where questions about individual and universal consciousness have been explored for centuries.

Why BIHS Keeps Returning

For BIHS, participation in conferences such as TSC is not about creating a separate conversation. It is about entering the existing scientific and philosophical conversation with arguments, research questions, and perspectives that can be examined alongside others.

The goal is not isolation. The goal is dialogue with rigor.

What Happens Next?

Course access is currently activated personally by our BIHS team. Here is what you can expect.

1

Enrollment Received

2

Canvas Access

We activate your access to the appropriate BIHS course or courses.

3

Start Learning

You receive your access information by email and can begin studying.

Please allow up to 1–2 business days for course access. Enrollment is currently activated manually by our BIHS team.

Your Canvas invitation will be sent to the email address associated with your enrollment. If you would like us to use a different email address for Canvas, please contact us at monicad@bihs.org.
